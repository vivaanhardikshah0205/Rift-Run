What do I do?

You run forward by yourself. Use the buttons to stay safe.

+

Collect these

Round gold coins buy upgrades.
Clear diamonds count toward your mission.

!

Avoid these

Flying targets, barriers and holes can hurt you. Move aside, jump or slide.

↑ ↑ ↑

One road, three lanes

Left and Right move between the three lanes. Everything you see is on this road. Collect treasure and dodge danger.

↗

Move and dodge

Use the big Left, Right, Jump and Slide buttons. On a phone you can also swipe.
On a keyboard: ← → move, Space jumps, ↓ slides.

◆

Finish your mission

Your goal is at the top. Run into diamonds and supplies to pick them up. Missed one? More will appear. Coins fill your Boost and buy upgrades after the run.

⌖

Beat the targets

Auto-shoot is on at first, so the Shoot button stays out of your way. Move into a target’s lane to aim. Stun stops nearby targets. Boost protects you when charged.
Keyboard: F shoot, C stun, Q boost. Stun shows a countdown; Boost lights up at 100%.

Bosses: move away from glowing attack lanes. Line up with the boss target and shoot when its shield opens.
Controller: stick / D-pad moves, A jumps, B slides, Y stuns, RT shoots, RB boosts, Menu pauses.
Pause shows your full goal and lets you change auto-shoot, sound or graphics.
